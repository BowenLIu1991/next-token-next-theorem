## Thread

1/5 The workflow: outline → one slide at a time → screenshot at 1280×720 → fix overflow → next. I reviewed every page visually. The model handled HTML/CSS/MathML; I handled the story.

2/5 The hard parts were layout and correctness: MathML that renders, a 45-slide deck with no overflow, two languages with the same structure, and a full-bleed PDF. Chrome kept shrinking the slides to ~88%, so the model wrote a screenshot-and-stitch pipeline.

3/5 I checked claims that sounded right. Some needed correction: AlphaFold is supervised learning; IMO 2025 is “gold-level”; OpenAI's 372-problem batch has 3 withdrawals and peer review is still open. Efficiency and accuracy are different tests.

4/5 The thesis: compression explains where knowledge comes from; verification and search explain why a model can solve problems. The same shift is reaching algorithm engineers: the job moves from writing the answer to directing the search.

5/5 If you teach or give technical talks, I'd love your feedback — especially on what to cut. If you want the outline or the HTML template, tell me in the comments.
